Support

Reminders don't alert me.
Open Dotdone → ⋯ → Settings and check that Notifications are On. On Android, also allow Alarms & reminders, and exclude Dotdone from battery optimisation if your phone maker restricts background apps.
Location reminders don't fire.
Allow location access (on Android choose Allow all the time). Alerts use the system's place monitoring, so they may arrive a minute or two after you arrive or leave.
Where is my data?
Only on your device. Dotdone has no account and no servers. Uninstalling the app deletes its data.
