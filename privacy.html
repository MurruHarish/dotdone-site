Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Dotdone is a reminders app that works entirely on your device. We built it so that we never see your data: there is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The short version

Dotdone does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

We do not use third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.

What the app stores on your device

Reminders, lists, groups, sections, notes, links, tags, priorities, dates and repeat rules that you create.

Photos you choose to attach to a reminder. The app keeps a private copy in its own storage so the attachment keeps working; it never reads other photos in your library.

Places you choose for location-based reminders (a name, an address and coordinates).

This data lives in the app's private storage and is not accessible to us. It is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall Dotdone.

Location

Dotdone uses your location only for features you turn on: picking your current location for a reminder, and alerting you when you arrive at or leave a place.

Arrive and leave alerts are monitored by your operating system (Core Location on iOS, Google Play services geofencing on Android). On Android this requires the "Allow all the time" permission so alerts work when the app is closed. Location is used only to trigger these reminders and is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

When you search for a place, the search text is resolved by your device's built-in geocoding service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android), which is governed by that provider's privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminder alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved and no notification content leaves your device.

Backups

If you have device backups turned on (iCloud Backup on iOS, Google backup on Android), your operating system may include the app's data in those backups, under your account and the provider's terms. We have no access to these backups.

Deleting your data

Delete any reminder or list in the app. Deleted reminders stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days and are then removed permanently; you can also remove them sooner from Recently Deleted.

Uninstalling Dotdone deletes all of its data from your device.

Children

Dotdone is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data (for example by adding optional sync), we will update this policy and the date above, and describe the change in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
